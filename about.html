About Al-Qaswa Aqua Viva

Al-Qaswa Aqua Viva (SMC-PVT.) Ltd. is a trusted bottled drinking water company based in Islamabad, Pakistan. We deliver pure, safe and mineral-enriched water to homes, offices and institutions across Rawalpindi and Islamabad. Our water undergoes advanced RO purification and is regularly tested to meet WHO and PSQCA standards.

Our Story

Al-Qaswa Aqua Viva was founded to provide a dependable source of high-quality drinking water in the twin cities. Our mission is to combine modern purification technology with accountability and timely service.
